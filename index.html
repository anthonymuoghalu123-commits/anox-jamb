About ANOX Prep

ANOX Prep is a modern learning and examination-preparation platform designed to help students prepare smarter, practice consistently, and build confidence for important examinations such as JAMB and WAEC.

The app brings practice questions, JAMB novels, WAEC practice, score tracking, and other useful study tools together in one simple and easy-to-use experience. Our goal is to make preparation more organized, accessible, and less stressful for students.

ANOX Prep is developed and maintained by ANOX Devs, a technology and software development team focused on building useful digital products that solve real problems. We believe technology should make learning easier, and ANOX Prep is built with that vision in mind.

We are continuously improving the app, adding new features, refining the experience, and listening to feedback from our users. ANOX Prep is only the beginning of what ANOX Devs aims to build.
